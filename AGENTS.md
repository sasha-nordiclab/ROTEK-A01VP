## Task and environment

- The project contains a reference model of the ROTEK WPDC-06.7L-10M-24-VP pump (PUM409) and a TPU 95A holder that is glued by its floor to the bottom of the bath, under water. The pump's mounting plate snaps into the holder; the goals are vibration isolation and easy removal.
- Working model: `cad/TPU_Pump_Holder.FCStd`, opened in FreeCAD 26.3. `scripts/build.py` describes the full rebuild procedure; every size lives in the `params` spreadsheet inside FreeCAD.
- Read and change geometry in the open FreeCAD **through the MCP `fdmkit.run(expr)`**, following the parent `../AGENTS.md`. The camera may be moved freely while working. After all operations, return to an axonometric overall view of the final model with Fit All through the MCP; clear only temporary selections made by the agent. Do not export STL/STEP/3MF unless asked.
- Before any full rebuild, check the open document for unsaved changes. `scripts/build.py` started from step zero closes the document and overwrites the FCStd.
- All committed text is English only (see `../AGENTS.md`).

## Design and editing rules

- Coordinate system: the centre of the four slots on the mounting face of the plate is (0,0,0); X along the pump axis, inlet towards −X; Z towards the pump. Print the holder floor down. There are no side walls, lips or separate entry keys any more.
- Change sizes in `params`; the geometry is built from native Pad, Chamfer, Fillet, MultiTransform, AdditiveLoft and Hole. The complete post is mirrored as a whole, so the posts never need editing one by one. After an edit check that sketches are fully constrained, bodies are valid, and intersection and fit are right.
- Material: AzureFilm TPU 95A. Body colours are saturated pastels: pump light blue `(0.51, 0.74, 0.96)`, holder coral `(0.98, 0.64, 0.51)`. Print profile TPU95A Fine, 100 % zig-zag; slicing and the physical fit are untested.

- The user sets the next geometric step. Do not change functional sizes on the basis of an assumption alone.
- The user's global CAD preference: native fully constrained sketches; first symmetry, equality, tangency, coincidence, horizontal and vertical constraints, then a minimal independent set of named dimensions. Do not fix geometry wholesale with Block/Fixed and do not clutter sketches with repeated coordinates. After an edit check DoF=0, no conflicts, and that the original geometry is preserved unless changing it was the goal.
